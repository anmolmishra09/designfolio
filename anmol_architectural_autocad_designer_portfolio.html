06 / METHODOLOGY

How I Work

A methodical six-step drafting protocol delivering predictability and zero surprises on site.

PHASE 01

Understand

Understand client requirements, project scope, spatial programs, municipal codes, and structural constraints before laying down the first digital baseline.

PHASE 02

Measure

Review real physical dimensions, perimeter surveys, existing structural columns, plumbing cores, and site orientation to establish true digital coordinates.

PHASE 03

Plan

Develop space planning, circulation hierarchies, room dimensions, natural light pathways, and logical drawing layer hierarchies.

PHASE 04

Draft

Convert the approved concept into precise 2D AutoCAD drawings with clean geometry, standardized line weights, dynamic blocks, and exact snap points.

PHASE 05

Detail

Add clear dimensional strings, level markers, material hatches, schedule tables, door/window tags, and section callouts.

PHASE 06

Review

Run full dimensional reconciliation, audit drawing scale factors, check revision histories, and export clean, plot-ready PDF sheets.
